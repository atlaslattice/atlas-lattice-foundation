# REGEN OLYMPICS — Regenerative World Tournament Archive

This directory tracks iterative tournament models for the Regenerative Kardashev / REGEN_COMPOUNDING framework.

## Versions
- [v0.5 — Dream Team Evolution](./v0.5/README.md): 5,000-world search, specialist champions, Pareto selection, Dream Team synthesis, championship replay.
- [v0.6 — Generational Dream Team Evolution](./v0.6/README.md): 512 worlds/generation, 100 generations, elite retention, crossover, mutation, Dream Team injection, Hall of Fame.

## Archive policy
- **GitHub:** indexed/executable canon, compact result tables, manifests, checksums, and links to raw large artifacts.
- **Google Drive:** full raw datasets and original PNG visualizations.
- **Notion:** knowledge/index layer with equations, interpretations, and cross-links.

## Interpretation boundary
All current outputs are toy systems simulations / hypothesis generators. They are not empirical forecasts of Earth or validated estimates of future planetary energy capacity.
