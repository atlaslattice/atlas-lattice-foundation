## Verified external evidence as of 2026-08-14

1. **Real 2026 laboratory result — electrochemical H/D isotope separation.**  
   A peer-reviewed JACS paper from Hunan University / collaborators reported room-temperature electrochemical hydrogen-isotope separation using hydrogen-bond-network engineering, with a gas-liquid separation coefficient of **17.28** and enrichment of a **5.0% D feed to 8.8% D after 4 h**. This is a real laboratory result. It is not a Stars 2.0 experiment and does not demonstrate direct production of bare deuterons or spin-energy harvesting.

2. **Real Chinese seawater-resource program.**  
   In June 2026, China publicly reported progress in extracting strategic resources from seawater, including breakthroughs in theories/key technologies related to **deuterium, lithium, uranium, and other trace resources**. The same report stated that China had **167 desalination projects totaling 3.077 million tonnes/day** of capacity.

3. **Real 2026 Chinese desalination policy expansion.**  
   China issued a 2026 Seawater Desalination Industry Development Action Plan targeting total national desalination capacity above **4.5 million tonnes/day by 2030**, with more than 1.5 million tonnes/day of new capacity.

4. **Natural deuterium abundance baseline.**  
   CIAAW/VSMOW gives a deuterium amount fraction near **1.5574e-4** of hydrogen atoms, with deep seawater near that value. This supports the order-of-magnitude estimate of roughly **30–35 g of deuterium per cubic metre of seawater**.

## Important scientific-status distinction

### Verified / external

- Deuterium exists naturally in seawater.
- Electrochemical H/D isotope separation has been demonstrated in the laboratory.
- China has publicly reported deuterium-related seawater-resource R&D.
- Large desalination infrastructure and a national expansion plan exist.

### Simulation / project hypothesis

- **STARS-OCEAN-002 is a simulation, not a laboratory result.**
- The Stars 2.0 deuteron spin-harvest mechanism is not experimentally validated.
- The B-11 binder function and Si-28/C-13/Li-6 containment functions remain hypotheses.
- Electron recovery as an integrated Stars 2.0 energy channel remains a design hypothesis.
- Claimed EROI, RF-output, polarization, and industrial-scale values from DeepSeek runs are simulation outputs and must not be presented as measured results.

## Provenance principle

Preserve model-by-model contributions, timestamps, corrections, rejected calculations, and external references. Do not retroactively convert simulation results into lab results or external developments into evidence that Stars 2.0 has already been implemented.

## Why this record matters

The exchange produced a potentially important integrated systems concept: use existing desalination infrastructure as a host for deuterium-isotope extraction and ocean-remediation pilots, while developing the separate Stars 2.0 spin/electron-recovery experiment independently. These two branches can succeed or fail independently and should remain separately instrumented and separately validated until integration is justified by data.
